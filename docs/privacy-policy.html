Genzeb Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 September 2026 · App: Genzeb (Android, package com.nitsuh.genzeb)

In short
  • Genzeb reads SMS messages on your phone only, to find transaction messages from recognised Ethiopian banks and mobile-money wallets.
  • Your SMS text never leaves your device. Personal (non-financial) SMS are ignored and not stored.
  • If you prefer not to grant SMS access, you can still add transactions manually.
  • Your financial records are stored in a database on your device. We do not have a copy.
  • You do not need an account. Optional Google sign-in (for a future paid plan) shares only your email, name, profile picture link and plan status with our account provider.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking SDKs. We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Genzeb ("we", "us") is a personal-finance app that helps people in Ethiopia understand their spending and income. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. Questions: CONTACT_EMAIL.

2. SMS messages

Permissions used: READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS.

Why: Genzeb's core feature is SMS-based money management. Banks and wallets in Ethiopia (for example Commercial Bank of Ethiopia, telebirr and other recognised financial senders) notify you of transactions by SMS. Genzeb reads these messages to build your transaction history, balances and budgets automatically, without you typing anything in.

How:

  • Genzeb only asks for SMS access after showing an in-app explanation, and only if you agree. You can use the app without granting it and add transactions manually.
  • Messages are processed entirely on your device by a built-in parser. SMS text is never uploaded, shared, or sent to us or to any third party (including any AI service).
  • Only messages from recognised financial senders are processed. Messages from other senders (personal conversations, OTPs from non-financial services, promotions) are ignored and not stored.
  • For a recognised financial SMS, Genzeb stores the message (sender, text, time) on your device together with the transaction details it extracts, such as amount, date, type (debit/credit), fees, balance and counterparty, so it can show you the source of each entry and re-check it later.
  • RECEIVE_SMS is used so new transaction messages appear while the app is open.

You can revoke SMS access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Genzeb → Permissions.

3. Data stored on your device

Transactions, categories, budgets, preferences and app settings are stored in a local database and local preferences on your phone. This data:

  • is not uploaded to our servers or any cloud;
  • is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer (the app disables Android backup), so it will not be restored if you reinstall the app or change phones;
  • is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall Genzeb.

If you use the app's share or export feature, the file is created on your device and sent only where you choose.

4. Optional account (Google sign-in)

An account is not required. If you choose to sign in with Google (used to manage a paid plan), we use Supabase as our authentication and account provider. The following is stored with Supabase:

DataPurpose
Email addressIdentify your account
Name and profile picture link (from your Google profile)Show who is signed in
Account ID and plan / subscription statusUnlock paid-plan features you are entitled to

Your SMS, transactions and other financial records are not linked to or uploaded with your account. Google processes the sign-in itself under Google's Privacy Policy. Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

You can sign out at any time. To delete your account and the data above, open Genzeb → Profile → Delete account; deletion is immediate and permanent. If you no longer have the app, email CONTACT_EMAIL from the address you signed in with and we will delete it within 30 days.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising ID.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not use it for credit scoring, lending or marketing.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, call log, camera, microphone or photos.

6. Sharing

We share data only with Supabase (and Google, for sign-in) as described in section 4, and only if you choose to sign in. We may disclose account data if required by law. Otherwise, nothing is shared.

7. Security

Financial data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Account data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with Supabase. Please protect your phone with a screen lock, since anyone with access to your unlocked phone can open the app.

8. Children

Genzeb is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Deny or revoke SMS permission at any time; the app continues to work with manual entry.
  • Delete all on-device data by clearing app data or uninstalling.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of account data by emailing CONTACT_EMAIL.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date, and where the change is significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect.
